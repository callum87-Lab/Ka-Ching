Notification settings

Ka-Ching! checks once a day for anything releasing tomorrow and sends a single push through whichever service you set up below, grouped by shop. Stays silent on days with nothing due.

POSTs this as JSON to the URL above, with {title} and {message} swapped for the real text — works with Discord, Slack, Home Assistant, Node-RED, or any custom script that can accept a webhook.

Save your settings first, then use the buttons above to confirm it actually works before relying on it. "Test tomorrow's digest" sends the real message you'd get tomorrow (or says "nothing releasing" if the day's quiet).
