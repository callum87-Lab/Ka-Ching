Add items

Set the release date and shop once, then add every comic from that same order before submitting — one shipment, one form. The order number and shipping cost are both optional, but adding them lets that shop's shipping figure be exact rather than an estimate.

Import order history

Copy your order history page (Forbidden Planet, eBay, or similar) and paste the whole thing below — addresses, card details and pagination are ignored automatically. Items you've already ticked "paid" or "cancelled" yourself are left alone; everything else gets refreshed to match what the retailer's currently showing, so it's safe to paste the same page again next month. You can also paste one of Forbidden Planet's "revised release date" emails straight in — it'll update just that item's date without needing a full re-import.
