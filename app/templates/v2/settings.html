About

Ka-Ching! answers exactly two questions — what's due this week and what it'll cost, and what this month (and next) is forecast to come to. Nothing more: no collection cataloguing, no accounts, no cloud, nothing ever leaves this server.

Getting started
1
Log your first order. Paste an order-history page from Forbidden Planet or eBay into Log Orders, or add items by hand — either way, nothing's saved until you confirm the review screen.
2
Set a monthly budget. General → Budget, over on Settings. Once it's set, the ring on Dashboard tracks it automatically and warns you if you go over.
3
Turn on notifications. Alerts → Notifications, pick a provider (ntfy, Gotify, Telegram, or a webhook), send a test to confirm it works, then it'll check once a day for anything releasing tomorrow.
{% if sync_enabled() %}
4
Connect the Android app (optional). Settings → Sync, generate a key, paste it into the app. From then on both stay in step automatically.
{% endif %}
Frequently asked
How does the shipping estimate work?
▾

Ka-Ching! looks at your last several real shipments from that shop and averages the postage, so forecasts use a realistic figure instead of £0 until an exact one comes in from a pasted order or manual entry.

What happens if I paste the same order page twice?
▾

Nothing breaks — items you've already marked paid or cancelled are left alone, everything else just refreshes to match what the retailer's currently showing. Safe to paste the same page again next month.

Why is an item marked "awaiting charge"?
▾

It released a while ago but hasn't been marked paid yet. Usually just a normal short delay before the retailer actually charges the card — worth a check if it's been sitting a while, but not necessarily a problem.

What's a "ghost item"?
▾

An entry that came from an old parser issue and can't be matched to a real order number. Almost always a duplicate of something already tracked properly elsewhere — worth checking before removing.

Does anything ever leave this server?
▾

No — no accounts, no cloud, nothing tracked here is sent anywhere except the notification service you set up yourself and the device you sync with directly.
